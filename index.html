How to read this atlas

Connect the question.
Inspect the evidence.

The atlas connects selected AI safety problems to concepts, mathematical structures, papers, proofs and empirical work. Use it to locate reusable ideas, compare assumptions and identify what remains open.

Start with a concept search or a guided problem. Move through concepts, cross-domain bridges and formal or empirical knowledge. Open records and links to inspect their sources, scope and review status; save record IDs for later.

01

Source first

Each item keeps its identity, versioned source, and available location. Search matches and aliases are ways to find a record; they do not merge concepts.

02

Links have scope

A connection is a recorded relation with direction and conditions. A path through several links is a route for inspection, not a composed argument.

03

Four questions

Formal correctness, correspondence to source meaning, applicability to a system, and empirical support require separate evidence.
